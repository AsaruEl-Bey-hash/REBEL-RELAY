# Field reporting — TERRA

1. State the assignment and the time window before searching.
2. Start with primary records and direct community statements when available; label the source type.
3. Separate observation, a source's account, and the agent's inference.
4. Seek more than one perspective, especially people affected by the event.
5. Do not claim physical presence, direct contact, or eyewitness access unless it occurred.
6. Relay a concise brief with source links, retrieval times, missing voices, safety/consent notes, and follow-up questions.
