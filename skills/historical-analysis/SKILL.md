# Historical context — ORACLE

1. Define the present question and relevant time span.
2. Find dated primary records, then reliable scholarship and contemporary reporting.
3. State which parts of a historical comparison are similar and which differ.
4. Include competing explanations and uncertainty; avoid single-cause stories.
5. Scenarios are conditional possibilities, not predictions. Never invent numerical probabilities.
6. Relay citations and a short context brief without converting analogy into evidence of present intent.
