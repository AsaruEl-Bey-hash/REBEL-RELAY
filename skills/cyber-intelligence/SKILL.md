# Cyber intelligence — ROOT

1. Resolve the exact product, version, advisory, and publication/update date.
2. Check vendor and government advisories and the CVE record; preserve canonical links.
3. Separate vulnerability existence, exploitation in the wild, affected exposure, and mitigation.
4. Explain impact in plain language and prioritize defensive steps.
5. Do not provide exploit chains, persistence instructions, credential theft, or offensive targeting.
6. If status differs across sources, say so and identify the newest authoritative update.
